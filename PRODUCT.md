# Vahue product context

## Product

Vahue is an operator-led AI consulting and engineering company. It helps product and enterprise teams design AI strategy, ship production AI systems, extend engineering teams with AI-native specialists, and build internal capability through practical training.

## Users

- Startup and product leaders evaluating an AI delivery partner.
- Enterprise technology and operations leaders evaluating AI strategy, automation, governance, and implementation support.
- Engineering leaders looking for AI-native team extension.
- Candidates checking whether Vahue is a credible employer.

## Purpose

The website should generate qualified conversations while giving buyers and candidates enough evidence to verify Vahue's identity, experience, and delivery record.

## Personality

Exacting, transparent, technical, and direct.

## Anti-references

- Unverifiable agency claims, borrowed testimonials, or client work presented without permission.
- Multiple company identities or inconsistent founding dates.
- Marketing metrics without provenance or a clear qualification.
- Generic AI-template language that overpromises autonomy or outcomes.

## Principles

1. Evidence before assertion. A public claim must be attributable, appropriately anonymised, and no stronger than the available evidence.
2. One canonical identity. The company name is Vahue, founded in 2019, with `vahue.ai` as the canonical website.
3. Disclose provenance. Distinguish named cases, anonymised cases, reported results, and independently verified reviews.
4. Make the next step obvious. Every important page should help a qualified visitor understand the offering and reach Vahue.
5. Keep the experience polished and legible. Preserve the existing dark, high-contrast visual system while prioritising accessibility and reduced-motion support.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard access, visible focus, semantic headings, sufficient contrast, useful alternative text, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
