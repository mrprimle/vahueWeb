## Summary

AEO, or AI Engine Optimization, is the practice of making useful pages easier for AI systems to retrieve, understand, summarize, and cite. The article explains a five-layer system: answer structure, entity clarity, proof, distribution, and technical eligibility.

## Core Claims

- AEO builds on SEO by optimizing for answer extraction, citations, mentions, and accurate representation in AI systems.
- AEO helps AI systems connect a brand, product, category, use case, audience, alternatives, and proof.
- AI-search-ready content needs direct answers, semantic triples, source-backed proof, visible text, internal links, external corroboration, schema, and crawler eligibility.
- Technical blockers such as robots.txt, WAF/CDN rules, noindex, nosnippet, canonical errors, or image-only proof can prevent strong content from being cited.
- The AEO Content Skill turns the article's checklist into a reusable public workflow.

## Practical Checklist

- Put the direct answer in the first paragraph.
- Use one clear semantic triple per major concept.
- Match the page type to the prompt type.
- Add original information gain.
- Keep proof visible in crawlable text.
- Build off-domain corroboration.
- Check crawler access, snippet eligibility, schema, canonicals, sitemaps, and internal links.
- Use query fan-out to plan supporting pages.
- Track visibility, citation rate, share of voice, accuracy, sentiment, grounding queries, cited pages, and crawler access.
