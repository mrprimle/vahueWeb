# Insurance — Claims Paperwork Into Clean Records, Run by Agents

Page: https://vahue.ai/industries/insurance

## What this is
Vahue deploys AI agents that turn notice-of-loss forms, claim files, correspondence and policy wordings into structured records in the insurer's core system, so adjusters review exceptions instead of keying documents. Every decision is cited, logged and reviewed by the insurer's own people.

## Problem it solves
- **Paperwork overload** — ACORD forms, PDFs and emails slow every workflow and keep key details out of reach of the insurer's systems.
- **Quiet claims leakage** — only a fraction of files gets reviewed, so leakage on subrogation, fraud and duplicate claims goes unseen.
- **No audit trail** — AI-assisted decisions leave no consistent record of why they were made.

## Workflows Vahue runs
- **First notice of loss intake agents** — turn forms, photos, emails and call notes into a structured claim file in the core system.
- **Claims correspondence** — draft replies to claimants, brokers and repairers from the claim file, with anything outside the agent's limits handed to a person.
- **Treaty and policy wording review** — answer coverage questions from policies and treaty wordings with the clause cited, so underwriters check instead of search.
