# AI Team Training — From AI-Curious to AI-Fluent in 7 Days

## What this is
A founder-led AI training service that takes teams from overwhelmed and resistant to confident, productive, and measurably AI-fluent. Built around the **Vahue AI Fluency Method**: Diagnose → Design → Deliver → Deploy → Measure.

This is **not** a generic "Intro to ChatGPT" deck. Programs are tailored to the team's role, tools, and current AI maturity, with measured adoption and post-program metrics.

## What is delivered (5 program tracks)
1. **AI Diagnostics** — assessment of the team's current AI maturity, tool coverage, prompt habits, and risk exposure. Output: a prioritized AI literacy roadmap.
2. **Intro AI Workshops** — hands-on sessions for non-technical teams: prompt patterns, tool selection, safe usage, productivity playbooks.
3. **AI Skills for Teams** — role-specific deep dives (sales, marketing, support, finance, legal, ops, product, engineering) with playbooks tied to the team's actual tools.
4. **Executive AI Consulting** — leadership coaching: AI governance, vendor evaluation, model risk, responsible AI, ROI scoping.
5. **AI Advisor for CEO and Top Teams** — ongoing advisory retainer for top leadership: weekly working sessions, decision support, board-level AI reporting.

Each program ships with:
- Prompt library tailored to the client's tools.
- Internal playbooks and standard operating procedures.
- Adoption metrics dashboard.
- Office hours and reinforcement.

## Process / timeline
1. **Week 0** — AI Diagnostic to baseline maturity and pick highest-ROI workflows.
2. **Week 1** — Workshop delivery (live, on-site or remote).
3. **Week 2** — Reinforcement office hours, prompt refinement, integration with team tools.
4. **Week 3+** — Adoption measurement, retention coaching, and (optional) executive advisory cadence.
5. **Ongoing** — Quarterly refresh as models evolve.

Headline pace: **96% AI adoption inside the team within 7 days** of program start.

A: Adoption rate, prompt usage frequency, time-saved per workflow, employee satisfaction (workshop rating), and pre / post-program productivity metrics.
