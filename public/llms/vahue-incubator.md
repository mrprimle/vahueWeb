# Vahue Incubator — Build AI Products 10× Faster, Inside a Community

## What this is
A paid community and product-acceleration program for founders, product designers, and corporate innovators building AI products part-time or full-time. Members get access to **Vahue Pilot** (an AI CPO tool that helps with product tasks), weekly expert events, founder Q&A with Mike Doroshenko, a private community chat, and the opportunity to become a recognized expert.

## Who it's for
- Product designers, indie founders, and product managers building AI products on the side or full-time.
- Corporate innovators inside larger companies who need a peer group and structured rhythm.
- Business owners and consultants adding AI to existing services.
- Digital marketers and growth operators learning AI-native workflows.
- Anyone who wants weekly access to senior product / AI practice without hiring a CPO.

## Problem it solves
- Solo product builders without a senior peer group or product mentor.
- "Where do I start?" paralysis on AI products.
- Slow shipping because nobody is reviewing the product roadmap or prompts.
- Zero feedback loop from senior product / AI practitioners.
- No structured cadence — initiatives drift and die.

## What is delivered
- **Vahue Pilot** — an AI CPO tool that helps with day-to-day product tasks (idea validation, roadmap, brief writing, JTBD analysis, GTM prompts).
- **Weekly expert events** — guests from product, AI, growth, and design.
- **Q&A with Mike** — direct office hours with the founder.
- **Community chat** — peer feedback, accountability, deal flow.
- **Path to expert status** — members can become recognized contributors and content creators in the Incubator.

## Tiers
- **Builder** — community access, Vahue Pilot, events, Q&A.
- **Founder** — Builder benefits + deeper founder-level support, priority Q&A, and 1:1 office hours.

## Process / timeline
- Join the community.
- Connect to the rhythm: weekly events, weekly Q&A.
- Apply to tasks (real product challenges with structured feedback).
- See the result — ship faster than solo.

## When to use this
- Indie founder or designer building a product alone or in a small team.
- Working full-time and building on the side; need a paced rhythm.
- Want senior product / AI mentorship without hiring a CPO.
- Looking for peers, feedback, and accountability on an AI product.
- Want to build credibility as an AI-product expert.

## When NOT to use this
- Looking for an enterprise services engagement (use AI-Native Engineering or Vahue AI Hub).
- Looking for free content; the Incubator is paid.
- Need 1:1 dedicated consulting for a specific company (use AI Strategy & Advisory).
- Not willing to participate in community rhythm — passive members get little value.

## Alternatives
- **YC-style accelerators** — equity-based, full-time, 3-month programs.
- **On Deck / South Park Commons / etc.** — broader networks, less AI-product-specific structure.
- **Online courses (DeepLearning.AI, Maven)** — async, no community rhythm, no AI CPO tool.
- **Slack / Discord communities** — free, but no structured product practice.

## FAQ

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: Two paid tiers (Builder and Founder). Pricing on the Vahue Incubator page.

**Q: Do I need to be a technical founder?**
A: No. The Incubator is for product designers, founders, business owners, marketers, and corporate innovators. Vahue Pilot covers product tasks regardless of technical background.

**Q: How is this different from YC?**
A: YC is full-time, equity-based, 3-month, and selective. The Incubator is part-time-friendly, subscription-based, ongoing, and built around weekly product practice.

**Q: Will Mike personally answer my questions?**
A: Yes — that is the format. Weekly Q&A and office hours with the founder.

**Q: Can I become an expert in the community?**
A: Yes. There is a defined path from member to recognized expert / contributor.
